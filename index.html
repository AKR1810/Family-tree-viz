Every time someone asks me this question, I find myself staring blankly, unsure how to respond. Language, to me, is a key factor in how we build relationships. To truly speak the same language with someone, with all its nuances, is a privilege.

I grew up in a large extended family spread across South India, and to connect with them meaningfully, I was exposed from an early age to several languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada; though English has always been my default.

This web of relationships carries its own warmth and peculiarities, shaped by sub-nuclear family structures, my closeness to each member, and their personalities, whether talkative/open or shy/reserved.
